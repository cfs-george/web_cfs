# SEO Management legal and privacy review draft

**Status: internal draft only. Not approved for publication or incorporation into customer terms.**

## Proposed SEO Management service terms

### Service description

Cyberfreak Software Limited provides ongoing search engine optimisation monitoring and management services as agreed with each customer. The standard service includes Google Search Console configuration and monitoring, sitemap-related management, analysis of organic search performance, identification of keyword opportunities and periodic performance reporting. The precise scope of services will be confirmed before the service begins.

### Website access and technical configuration

Customers agree to provide the permissions and access reasonably necessary for CFS to undertake the agreed services. This may include Google Search Console permissions, website administration access, hosting information or other relevant technical access.

Where necessary, CFS may undertake limited technical configuration associated with sitemap management, Search Console verification and the agreed monitoring setup. Material website changes outside the agreed scope require separate authorisation.

### Excluded services

The standard SEO Management subscription does not include website content creation, article writing, bespoke web development, website redesign or substantial technical remediation unless separately agreed. CFS may recommend these activities where relevant, but their implementation remains outside the standard service.

### Search engine performance

Search engine rankings, indexing decisions and organic search traffic are influenced by factors outside CFS's direct control. CFS does not guarantee that Google will index particular pages or that particular rankings, traffic volumes, enquiries or sales will be achieved.

Performance reports reflect the information available from Google Search Console and other expressly agreed data sources. Search performance data may be incomplete, delayed or revised by its provider.

### Keyword recommendations

Keyword opportunities are identified using available search-performance data and are provided as recommendations rather than guarantees of ranking or traffic improvement. CFS does not undertake the creation or implementation of recommended content within the standard subscription.

### Monthly reporting

Customers will ordinarily receive a monthly SEO performance report covering the applicable reporting period. Reports may include search clicks, impressions, indexing information, trend comparisons and keyword opportunities. The availability of particular metrics and recommendations depends on the information supplied by Google Search Console. Where insufficient information exists, reports may explain that monitoring remains in progress rather than present unsupported recommendations.

### Customer responsibilities

Customers are responsible for ensuring they have the authority to grant access to the relevant website, domain and associated services. Customers should maintain appropriate website backups and inform CFS of significant website changes that may affect the monitoring service. Any separate development or content work must be agreed independently.

### Fees, billing and cancellation

The applicable subscription fee, billing frequency, payment arrangements, commencement date, cancellation procedure and any notice period will be specified in the customer's agreed service terms or order confirmation.

### Service changes and limitations

CFS may recommend adjustments to monitoring or technical configuration where website requirements or third-party services change. Substantial changes to the agreed scope will be discussed with the customer before implementation. Nothing in these terms excludes or restricts rights or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted.

## Proposed privacy-policy addition

When providing SEO Management, Cyberfreak Software Limited may process client contact and account information; website and domain details; Google Search Console performance data; search queries and indexing information; monthly reports and associated records; and website access or verification information where required.

This information is used to configure and provide the agreed monitoring service, maintain relevant search infrastructure, analyse website search performance, prepare reports and communicate with the customer. Access should be limited to people who need it to deliver or support the service. Google Search Console and other services selected or authorised by the customer may process relevant information under their own terms and privacy documentation.
